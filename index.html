The seasons

Summer

The summer is very hot and we have no school. We can go to the beach everyday and tan and play volleyball!

Fall

The fall is cool and we are back in school. The trees turn red and orange with crunchy leaves. We also have halloween and thanksgiving!

Winter

The winter is freezing cold, we have a bunch of tests but we get to have snow! You can go skiing or sledding and build snowmen.

Spring

In spring flowers bloom and we have lots of rain. You can go on picnics and look forward to school ending!
